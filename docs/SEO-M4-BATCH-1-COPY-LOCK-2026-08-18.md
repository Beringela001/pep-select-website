## 1. GLP-2T product description

### Description

> Tirzepatide is a peptide engineered to engage the GIP and GLP-1 receptors. Researchers study its dual-receptor binding profile and the structural features that shape signaling at each receptor.

- Dual GIP and GLP-1 receptor engagement
- Structural basis of dual-receptor binding
- Receptor-specific binding and signaling behavior

This copy replaces the current supplier paragraph beginning `GLP-2T 20MG. This product is intended exclusively for laboratory research...` and the associated generic Product Features and Storage text.
