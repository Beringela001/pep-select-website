## Status rules

- **Already complete:** preserve and monitor; do not reopen without contrary evidence.
- **Partially complete:** prior milestone work exists, but the audit identified a remaining gap or outcome.
- **Conflicting / validate:** the recommendation conflicts with verified implementation, supported-data rules, or another audit statement; evidence decides the action.
- **Blocked / input needed:** requires business data, records, account access, compliance approval, or a decision from Paulo.
- **Superseded / no immediate fix:** informational, optional, time-dependent, or not useful at current priority.
- **Not started:** valid candidate after ownership and dependency checks.

## Change control

Each status changes only when evidence is added: code/file reference, live URL response, GSC export, PageSpeed run, schema test, approved business record, or deployment verification. Live remains untouched until Paulo explicitly approves a deployment batch.
