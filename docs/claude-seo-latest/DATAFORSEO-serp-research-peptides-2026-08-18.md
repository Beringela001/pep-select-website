## What this means for Pep Select

1. **Trust language is the ranking currency.** Every winning vendor title uses "third-party tested", "99%+ purity", "COA", "US made" — and Google's own Purity Standards card mirrors that (HPLC >99%, COA, MS, Janoshik testing). Title tags and product pages should lead with verifiable purity/testing claims.
2. **AI Overview citations are open to vendors.** Two competitors are cited directly, and vendor blogs dominate the "Things to know" citations. Well-structured educational content (what research peptides are, purity standards, US legality/RUO labeling) is a realistic path into AIO and consideration cards.
3. **Merchant Center matters.** Two of nine organic listings arrive via Shopping-feed auto-tagging, and three show price annotations — product structured data plus a Merchant Center feed is table stakes here.
4. **PAA questions are a ready-made content map** — a "most trusted peptide company" trust page, a "do peptides work" evidence page, a "where to buy in the USA" page, and a risks/safety page each target one PAA slot.
5. **Compliance framing is non-negotiable.** Google layers heavy "not for human use" caution over this SERP; content that matches that RUO framing (like GenScript's) coexists with commercial rankings, while consumption-oriented framing would clash with it.
