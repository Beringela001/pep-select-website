# Competitor Boundaries

## General rule

Competitors may be used to identify customer expectations or missing functionality. Do not copy their copy, visual composition, naming, or claims.

## TrustedPeps

Do not reproduce TrustedPeps language, response patterns, page structure, or generic reassurance. Pep Select must differentiate through documented batch history, truthful status, accessible pricing, clear service, and organized customer flows.

## Premium-priced competitors

Do not imitate inflated luxury positioning or imply that higher price is evidence of quality. Pep Select can look refined without becoming exclusive or inaccessible.

## Supplier-adjacent resellers

Do not imitate bare storefronts, thin documentation, crowded product grids, or informal underground presentation. Fair pricing must coexist with quality control, support, and documentation.

## Visual-reference policy

When a competitor screenshot is supplied:

1. Identify what functional problem the reference solves.
2. Describe the transferable UX principle.
3. Produce a Pep Select-specific composition using the approved design system.
4. Avoid copying exact layout, text, color pattern, icons, or hierarchy.

## Copy boundary

Avoid interchangeable phrases such as:

- premium quality you can trust
- unmatched purity
- your trusted source
- research made simple
- excellence in every vial
- fast, reliable, and secure

Replace them with specific evidence, process, or action language.
