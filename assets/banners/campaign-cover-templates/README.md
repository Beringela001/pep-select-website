# Campaign cover design templates

These transparent overlays match the Campaign Cover Banner's two recommended master sizes.

| Asset | Canvas | Critical-content safe area | Safety margins |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Desktop | 1920 x 1080 | 1440 x 760 | 240 px sides; 160 px top/bottom |
| Mobile | 1080 x 1920 | 840 x 1200 | 120 px sides; 360 px top/bottom |

## Use

1. Create the campaign artwork at the full canvas size.
2. Place the matching SVG or PNG guide as the top layer.
3. Extend photography, color, and texture to every canvas edge.
4. Keep the logo, offer, dates, fine print, and essential product details inside the blue safe-content rectangle.
5. Hide or delete the guide layer before exporting the finished campaign image.

`Fill screen` can crop the shaded margins as browser proportions change. `Show full image` preserves the complete canvas but can leave background space around it.

The center crosshair matches a 50% horizontal and 50% vertical focal point. Move the campaign's visual subject around that point, or adjust the focal-point controls in WordPress for an intentionally off-center composition.
