Restricts checkout shipping to the contiguous 48 states and Washington, D.C.

== Description ==

Adds three safeguards:

* Removes shipping rates for excluded destinations and ZIP ranges.
* Prevents checkout submission for excluded destinations.
* Shows an accessible inline warning above the ZIP field.
