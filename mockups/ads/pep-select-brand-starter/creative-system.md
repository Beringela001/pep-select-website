# Pep Select Creative System

## Design direction

Use a restrained clinical-editorial look: real product or documentation imagery, generous white space, navy-led typography, cyan as a navigation accent, and minimal decoration. The creative should feel specific and inspectable.

## Layout rules

- Keep the logo inside the central safe zone and give it clear space equal to the height of the monogram’s inner stroke.
- Use one headline, one supporting sentence, and one CTA.
- Keep text under roughly 20 percent of the canvas when the placement benefits from visual-first delivery.
- Use a white card on the tinted surface. Do not stack filled cards.
- Use an 8px button radius and 6px inner-card radius when the ad includes interface-like surfaces.
- Keep Story/Reels text away from the top 250px and bottom 320px.

## Type hierarchy

- Editorial headline: Georgia Bold, 64–96px depending on placement.
- Interface copy: Plus Jakarta Sans Regular or Semibold, 28–38px.
- Technical labels and the research-use footer: IBM Plex Mono Medium, 20–26px.
- Use no more than two type families in one ad unless the technical footer needs IBM Plex Mono.

## Color use

- Navy `#002A53`: primary text and CTA.
- Dark navy `#001D3A`: hover or dark field.
- Cyan `#17A1CF`: accent line, small label, or secondary emphasis.
- Surface `#F3F8FC`: full-field background.
- White `#FFFFFF`: card background and reversed text.
- Amber, green, and red are status colors. Do not use them as decorative ad colors.

## Image rules

- Prefer the production hero, vial/batch, and COA assets listed in `asset-manifest.csv`.
- Preserve vial labels and packaging. Do not alter compound names, strengths, lot IDs, QR codes, cap colors, or crimp colors.
- Use COA images as evidence context. Do not pull a result into ad copy without current record-level approval.
- Avoid lifestyle or body imagery that could imply personal use.

## Starter placements

| Placement | Size | Primary job |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Meta feed | 1080 × 1350 | Product awareness with documentation angle |
| Square | 1080 × 1080 | Retargeting or archive discovery |
| Story/Reels | 1080 × 1920 | Fast batch-documentation message |
| Landscape | 1200 × 628 | Link ad or responsive display source |

## Publish checklist

- Confirm URL and CTA match.
- Confirm the product and packaging shown are current.
- Confirm every batch or laboratory statement against the current record.
- Include `For research use only.`
- Check contrast, safe zones, spelling, and logo clear space.
- Review the final creative at actual phone size.
- Record the campaign, variant, destination, and date in the filename or ad ledger.
