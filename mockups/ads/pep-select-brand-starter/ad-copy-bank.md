# Pep Select Ad Copy Bank

All copy below is a production starting point. Confirm the destination, selected product, batch status, availability, pricing, and supporting records before publication.

## Angle 1: Product first

**Audience state:** Knows the category and wants to browse.

- Headline: `A short list, on purpose.`
- Primary text: `Explore carefully selected research compounds with clear product details close at hand.`
- CTA: `Explore Compounds`
- Destination: Catalog

## Angle 2: Documentation first

**Audience state:** Wants evidence before deciding what to purchase for research.

- Headline: `What’s behind the label matters.`
- Primary text: `Review available batch documentation before you decide what belongs in your research workflow.`
- CTA: `Review COAs`
- Destination: Quality Archive

## Angle 3: Find the record

**Audience state:** Has a vial or compound in mind and wants the documentation.

- Headline: `Find the record behind the vial.`
- Primary text: `Search the Quality Archive by compound and open the batch documentation available for each release.`
- CTA: `View Testing History`
- Destination: Quality Archive

## Angle 4: Transparent catalog experience

**Audience state:** Frustrated by scattered product information.

- Headline: `Research without the runaround.`
- Primary text: `Current product details and available batch records stay close to the compound you’re reviewing.`
- CTA: `Explore Compounds`
- Destination: Catalog

## Required footer

`For research use only.`

## Copy review gate

- One audience, one message, one CTA.
- No medical, therapeutic, body-outcome, dosing, or administration language.
- No universal testing, purity, sterility, availability, fulfillment, or superiority claims.
- No invented numbers, testimonials, rankings, urgency, or scarcity.
- If copy names a product or batch, verify every factual detail against the current record.
